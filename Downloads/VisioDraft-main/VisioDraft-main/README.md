# VisioDraft 🧠📚

> **Empowering students with a smarter way to learn, share, and grow.**

VisioDraft is a dynamic and student-centric platform designed to simplify knowledge sharing across college and university campuses. With VisioDraft, students can upload their personal notes, access high-quality study materials, and ask academic queries powered by AI — all in one place.

---

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ **Student Login System** – Secure login and personalized dashboards.
- 📄 **Notes Uploading** – Students can upload their own handwritten or digital notes (PDF, images, etc.).
- 📥 **Download Materials** – Access free, quality educational content anytime.
- 🔎 **AI-Powered Query Assistant** – Get instant answers to subject-based questions (GPT integrated).
- 💬 **Community Driven** – Built for students, by students.
- 📂 **Categorized Content** – Organised by subject, topic, semester, and university.
- 📊 **Smart Analytics (Planned)** – Track popular notes, search trends, and more.
- 💡 **Future Add-ons** (Coming Soon):
  - Note selling feature with wallet integration.
  - Tutor-student collaboration boards.
  - Admin panel for university-level control.
